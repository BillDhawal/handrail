## KQ1. Which verticals have the highest "same product, many tenants" density, and for each: dominant products, API status, recurring screen tasks, who does them today, cost?

### Takeaway
Density (many tenants, same vendor product, slightly different config) is highest in property management (AppFolio alone: 22,096
customers on one product), auto dealerships (two DMS vendors that charge and litigate over third-party access), dental practices
(three PMS products, payer portals shared across every practice), independent insurance agencies (every agency re-keys into the same
~dozen carrier portals), and healthcare revenue cycle (every provider hits the same payer portals). Logistics, legal e-filing and
accounting have high task recurrence but more fragmented target systems (per-carrier, per-state, per-bank), which weakens cross-tenant
capability reuse unless the target is the shared portal rather than the firm's own system of record.

#### Auto dealerships (CDK, Reynolds & Reynolds, Tekion, Dealertrack; OEM, DMV, lender portals)
- CDK's own site: "1,000+ integrated partners in our open, dealer-ready ecosystem"; handles "2.6% of U.S. GDP" on behalf of dealer partners; no rooftop count given — [CDK Global](https://www.cdkglobal.com/).

- Authenticom v. CDK/Reynolds (filed May 2017): July 2017 preliminary injunction found evidence of a "per se illegal horizontal conspiracy" to block third-party data access; CDK settled Nov 2020 with a one-time cash payment — [Wikipedia CDK Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CDK_Global); [CDK FY2021 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609702/000160970221000058/cdk-20210630.htm).
- Tekion v. CDK (Dec 10, 2024): federal antitrust suit alleging CDK withholds/delays dealers' access to their own data to block switching; seeks data transfer within 10 days of request; cites Arizona DMS open-access law — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241210707655/en/Tekion-Files-Lawsuit-Against-CDK-Globals-Anticompetitive-Practices); CDK countersued over a "virtual tunnel"; a separate 2018 class action over data-access fees settled for $630M — [Car Dealership Guy](https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/tekion-sues-cdk-global-accuses-firm-of-holding-data-hostage-2024-12-10).
- June 19, 2024 ransomware outage: CDK paid $25M in bitcoin June 21; nearly all dealers back by July 4; ~$605M dealer losses in first two weeks; ≥8 negligence suits — [Wikipedia CDK Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CDK_Global). ## KQ5. Who is the buyer and what is the sales motion (direct to SMB, via MSP/VAR, or embedded in vertical SaaS)?

### Takeaway
Evidence points to three working motions: (1) direct to the practice/agency/PM owner priced per transaction like the BPO they replace (dental billing cos, Toothy, Infinitus); (2) through the outsourcing firm itself (VA agencies and BPOs already sell "AppFolio data entry" and "dental verification" as a service and are the natural wholesale buyer of a capability library); (3) embedded via the vertical SaaS marketplace where the platform is open (Procore Marketplace, RealPage AppPartner, Open Dental, Bullhorn Marketplace), but not where the platform monetises the API (Yardi, AppFolio, CDK).

### Cited Findings
- Dental verification is already bought per transaction from BPOs on month-to-month terms (30-day cancel, $299 setup) — [DentalBilling.com](https://dentalbilling.com/pricing-dental-insurance-verification/); Infinitus sells annual platform + per task with multi-year discounts — [Infinitus](https://www.infinitus.ai/pricing/).
- VA agencies package product-specific VAs ("AppFolio virtual assistant", "Amazon VA") with flat monthly plans — [Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/appfolio-data-entry-support), [VA Masters](https://vamasters.com/how-much-does-amazon-virtual-assistant-cost/), [HireTalent](https://hiretalent.ph/tool/appfolio).
- Platform marketplaces: Procore Marketplace Partner tier with certification, free — [Procore](https://developers.procore.com/documentation/content/verification-and-production-access); RealPage AppPartner/Exchange — [RealPage](https://www.realpage.com/exchange/); Buildium Marketplace open to all subscription levels **[search snippet]** — [Buildium UK pricing](https://learn.buildium.com/uk-ireland/pricing/); Yardi requires ≥3 mutual Voyager clients before partnership — [Supergood Yardi](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/yardi-voyager).
- Dealers: vendors' integration costs are "typically passed on to dealers" — [DealerRefresh](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/vendors/cdk-global/); Arizona's DMS law requires providers to open systems to third parties — [BusinessWire Tekion](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241210707655/en/Tekion-Files-Lawsuit-Against-CDK-Globals-Anticompetitive-Practices).
- Freight: Pallet sells outcome-priced POCs to brokerages/3PLs — [Pallet](https://www.pallet.com/).
- Hotels: Booking.com forces individual properties through channel managers, so the channel manager is the embedding point — [Booking.com](https://connect.booking.com/).

### Inferences
- Highest-leverage first buyer is the BPO/VA agency: it already owns hundreds of same-product tenants, prices per task, and bears the labour cost a capability eliminates; it can also act as the human rung of the escalation ladder.
- Direct-to-SMB works where the unit price is legible (per verification, per filing, per title); it is weak where the pain is diffuse (general "data entry").
- Embedded motion is only viable on open platforms; on gated ones (Yardi, CDK) the product must sit outside the platform and be sold on the owner's right to operate their own account.

### Gaps
- No sources found on MSP/VAR channels reselling RPA to SMBs, or on vertical-SaaS revenue-share terms for embedded agents (search budget). No independent count of US independent insurance agencies, dental practices by PMS, or freight brokerages was captured.
